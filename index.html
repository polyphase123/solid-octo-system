Evacuation Pathfinder Guide

Understanding the Room-Level Evacuation Pathfinder:

  1. 105 Individual Classroom Vertices: The graph now fully maps all classrooms (e.g. Room 304, Room 112) in EHS.
  2. Whole-Building Multi-Floor Layout: See all floors of the selected building stacked vertically with hallways and stairwells.
  3. Interactive Control Stepper: Manual controls with default 0.1-second autoplay speed.
  4. Reset Configuration Button: Instantly reverts simulator back to clear weather settings.
  5. Active Hazard Presets & blockages: Modify routes dynamically by triggering earthquake structures and fire blockages.
